# Springer erratum request, drafted 2026-09-21 (not yet sent)

**Chapter:** Kennedy, B. J., Mohammad, A. F., & Wyandt, M. (2026). Synthetic Cognitive Augmentation
Network. In *SEET 2025*, CCIS 2725, pp. 179–188. DOI 10.1007/978-3-032-08977-9_13.

**Route.** Springer Nature handles post-publication corrections to proceedings chapters through the
production contact, who forwards to the volume editors and the Springer books team; the outcome for
a reference-list error is a published Correction (erratum) linked to the chapter, since the
version of record is not edited in place. The production contact for this volume was Manigandan K
at Straive (`manigandank.kumarr@straive.com`), who sent the galley on 2025-11-11 under subject
"Your Proof in [978-3-032-08976-2, SEET 2025, CCIS 2725, paper approval (643787_1_En, Chapter 13)]".
Reply on that thread so the chapter identifiers travel with it, and copy the co-authors.

**What is wrong.** Reference 9 reads: "Zhang, S., Dean, J.C.: Spikeformers: Transformers with
Spiking Neural Networks. arXiv preprint arXiv:2109.12894 (2021)". No such work exists. arXiv:2109.12894
is Eshraghian et al., "Training Spiking Neural Networks Using Lessons From Deep Learning", and no
paper titled "Spikeformers" by those authors is indexed anywhere. The entry is cited once, in the
future-work paragraph on spiking transformers.

**Replacement.** The sentence is about spiking transformers as a direction, so the natural
replacement is the paper that introduced the term: Zhou, Z., Zhu, Y., He, C., Wang, Y., Yan, S.,
Tian, Y., Yuan, L.: Spikformer: When Spiking Neural Network Meets Transformer. In: ICLR 2023
(2023). arXiv:2209.15425. In-text "[9]" is unchanged.
