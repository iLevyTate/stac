thread so the chapter identifiers travel with it, and copy the co-authors.

numbers below are from the manuscript section structure; confirm them against the galley

Dear Mr. Manigandan,

I am writing about our published chapter in SEET 2025, CCIS 2725, chapter 13, "Synthetic
Cognitive Augmentation Network", DOI 10.1007/978-3-032-08977-9_13, pages 179 to 188, with my
co-authors in copy.

I would like to request a correction to three items, two in the reference list and one clause
in the body. I understand this will most likely be handled as a published Correction rather
than an edit to the chapter, and I am content with whichever route Springer uses.

1. Reference 9 reads:

     Zhang, S., Dean, J.C.: Spikeformers: Transformers with Spiking Neural Networks. arXiv
     preprint arXiv:2109.12894 (2021)

   No such paper exists. The arXiv identifier belongs to a different paper by different
   authors, and no work with that title is indexed anywhere. I should have caught this before
   approval and I am sorry that I did not. It is cited once, in Section 1.2, in a sentence
   about spiking transformers as a research direction, and that sentence is unaffected. The
   reference should read:

     Zhou, Z., Zhu, Y., He, C., Wang, Y., Yan, S., Tian, Y., Yuan, L.: Spikformer: When
     Spiking Neural Network Meets Transformer. In: International Conference on Learning
     Representations (ICLR 2023). arXiv:2209.15425 (2023)

   The in-text citation [9] does not change.

2. Reference 4 gives the wrong volume and year for a real paper. It reads "J. Neurosci.
   20(11), 5473-5481 (2000)" and should read "J. Neurosci. 19(13), 5473-5481 (1999)". Authors,
   title and pages are correct. The in-text citation [4] does not change.

3. In Section 2.2, one sentence reads: "Although SCANAQ has not yet been implemented, it has
   been validated through current research and will be a focus in future studies." The
   instrument draws its items from validated source scales but has not itself been through a
   validation study, so the sentence claims more than it should. It should read: "Although
   SCANAQ has not yet been implemented, it draws on validated source scales and will be a
   focus in future studies." This is the least important of the three; if it complicates
   the Correction, please apply the first two and leave it.

Please let me know if you need anything further from me, or if this should go to a different
contact.

With thanks,

Ben Kennedy
Capitol Technology University

bkennedy1@captechu.edu
