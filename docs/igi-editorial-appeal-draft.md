What is wrong, in brief.

  Chapter 1 has thirty-two items in four groups. Twelve are statements about the mechanism of a

  Chapter 2 has eight items, all in the reference list: five entries whose identifier resolves to
  an unrelated paper and for which no work with the cited title exists, two real works with the
  wrong identifier, and one dead DOI on an uncited entry.

  No published result in either chapter changes. Each defective reference supports a background
  sentence that the replacement supports equally. The mechanism corrections in chapter 1 affect
  no reported number, because the chapter reports none for that component.

bibliographical references after publication is not acceptable for reasons of ethical integrity."
